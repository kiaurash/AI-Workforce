# M0 Facilitator Guide — Opportunity Discovery and Workflow Safety

## Purpose

Help learners select one safe, independently valuable M1 workflow. M0 is not a workforce-design exercise and does not require an installed agent.

## Materials and saved paths

Require the Entry Ramp v1 evidence map:

- `discovery/opportunity-map.md`
- `discovery/workflow-safety-triage.md`
- `inputs/representative-input.md`
- `governance/cohort-one-action-boundary.md`

## Facilitation sequence

1. Ask for a real work episode, not an idealized AI department.
2. Ask what output would help a real human now.
3. Narrow to trigger → representative input → bounded work → draft/proposal output → human review.
4. Ask what should remain human-owned.
5. Complete safety triage before approving a representative input.
6. Require a cohort-one action boundary for operational workflows.

## Safety triage interventions

- **Confidential/regulated/client data:** require authorization, minimization, or anonymized representative input.
- **Financial, legal, safety, or people decisions:** narrow the M1 task to evidence preparation or drafting; retain decision and execution with the human.
- **External action:** prohibit live execution in cohort one; define the draft/proposal boundary.
- **Overbroad workflow:** insist on a single independently valuable output.

## Completion decision

Mark the learner **ready**, **ready with a stated gap**, or **not yet ready**. A learner is ready only when all four evidence files exist and the learner can explain why the M1 task is useful, bounded, and safe.

## Handoff to M0.5 and M1

M0.5 verifies Desktop, repository, and Conductor mechanics. M1 uses the M0 evidence without reopening discovery unless a safety issue or scope problem is found.
