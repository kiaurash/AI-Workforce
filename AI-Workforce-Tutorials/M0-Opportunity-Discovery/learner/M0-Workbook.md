## 1. Start with work, not agents

Describe one recent work episode that consumed attention or created delay.

- Who needs a better outcome?
- What happens now?
- What is the actual constraint?
- What output would be useful even if no larger workforce were ever built?

```text

```

For every consequential action, keep the first M1 task at research, extraction, analysis, or draft/proposal stage. Cohort one does not perform live sends, publication, purchases, authoritative-record updates, or production execution.
