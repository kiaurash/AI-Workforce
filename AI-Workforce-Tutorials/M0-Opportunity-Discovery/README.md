# M0 — Opportunity Discovery and Workflow Safety

**Format:** self-guided or facilitated preparation

**Output:** a bounded workflow ready for M0.5/M1—not a workforce design

## Learner outcome

The learner saves two files in the capstone repository:

- `discovery/opportunity-map.md`
- `discovery/workflow-safety-triage.md`

The learner then selects one safe representative input at `inputs/representative-input.md` and writes a learner-specific cohort-one action boundary at `governance/cohort-one-action-boundary.md`.

## Materials

- [Learner workbook](learner/M0-Workbook.md)
- [Facilitator guide](facilitator/M0-Facilitator-Guide.md)

## Completion gate

The learner can explain: the value of the chosen workflow; its trigger, input, and independently useful output; the human responsibilities retained; permitted representative input; prohibited live actions; and assumptions to test in M1.
