# Research Evidence Release Gate

## Status

**Evidence pending.** The Hermes-preferred course redesign may be developed as a draft, but it is not a research-final release until the NotebookLM evidence acceptance probe passes and the cited research brief is committed.

## Scope

This gate applies to claims about multi-agent role separation, handoff and provenance practices, citation requirements, learner risks, and any module-sequencing choice presented as research-derived.

It does not prohibit clearly labelled design decisions that have already been confirmed by the course sponsor.

## Required acceptance probe

Before a NotebookLM-dependent exercise, template, or design rationale is marked final, the research role must demonstrate all of the following with the assigned notebook:

1. Retrieve an answer to one relevant question from the assigned notebook.
2. Verify that the response includes usable source-level citation or provenance information.
3. Save an inspectable evidence artifact in the repository containing:
   - the question asked;
   - the response excerpt or faithful summary;
   - source-level citation or provenance returned by NotebookLM;
   - retrieval date;
   - known limitations or unresolved ambiguity.
4. Have the orchestrator review the artifact and record whether it is approved for course-design use.

A token-cache refresh or a successful sign-in indication is not sufficient evidence. The role must successfully retrieve and document notebook-grounded source information.

## Required repository artifacts

The research analyst must commit:

- a cited research brief; and
- a provenance template that learners can use in the evidence-brief handoff.

The course designer must then link research-grounded module claims to the approved brief or label them as design judgement.

## Draft labelling rule

Until the acceptance probe succeeds, all research-derived sequencing claims must be labelled **Evidence pending**. Do not replace unavailable NotebookLM findings with uncited synthesis while describing them as NotebookLM-grounded research.

## Final-release condition

The revised course can be marked final only when:

- the acceptance probe artifact is committed;
- the cited research brief and provenance template are committed;
- the module sequence identifies which claims depend on those sources; and
- the orchestrator has recorded approval for their use.
