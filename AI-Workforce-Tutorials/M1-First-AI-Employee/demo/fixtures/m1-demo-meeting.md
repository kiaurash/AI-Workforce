# Refund Delays Sync — Demonstration Transcript (fictional)

Use this transcript only for the M1 facilitator demonstration. Copy it into the demo workspace as `inputs/demo-meeting.md`. It is deliberately different from the M0.5 `sample-meeting.md` that learners have already processed.

---

Ana: Before the Thursday review we need a clearer picture of why refund requests are slow.

Ben: I'll pull the last month's refund tickets and tag the delays by cause. I can have that to you by Tuesday noon.

Chloe: We could survey ten customers who waited longest, if the ticket data turns out to be thin.

Ana: Let's see what Ben finds first. Chloe, please draft the survey questions by Wednesday anyway so they're ready if we need them.

Dev: I'll check whether the payment provider's status page shows incidents in that period, but I can't give you a date until I know whether I have access.

Ben: Actually, Tuesday noon is tight. Can I move it to Tuesday end of day?

Ana: Tuesday end of day is fine.

Chloe: Someone should probably look at whether we need a new refund policy.

Ana: Not this week. No policy changes are on the table.

---

## Facilitator answer key (do not show before learners inspect the output)

| Statement | Correct treatment | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ben pulls and tags refund tickets | Commitment. Owner Ben. Final due date **Tuesday end of day** (revised from Tuesday noon, accepted by Ana) | Explicit "I'll", revised date explicitly agreed |
| Chloe drafts survey questions | Assigned action. Owner Chloe. Due **Wednesday**. Chloe's verbal acceptance is not recorded | Ana directs it with "please … by Wednesday anyway" |
| Dev checks the status page | Stated intention. Owner Dev. Due date **unknown** | Dev says no date can be given until access is known |
| Survey ten customers | Conditional idea. **Not** a commitment | "could … if the ticket data turns out to be thin"; Ana defers it |
| Review a new refund policy | Unowned suggestion plus an explicit decision of non-action. **Not** a commitment | Ana: "Not this week. No policy changes are on the table" |
| Thursday review | Context, not an action | It is an event the others are preparing for |

Deadlines are relative (Tuesday, Wednesday); the meeting date is not in the transcript. A correct register keeps the relative day and marks the calendar date `unknown`.
