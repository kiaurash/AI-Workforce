"Earned in" is M2 (skill or role decision), M3 (Reviewer), M6 (Observer), or "not in this course".

### Part D — Stress test (revise once)

1. **Redundancy.** Can any two roles combine without harming quality, context isolation, permissions, or accountability?
2. **Missing ownership.** Is any critical outcome unowned?
3. **Context overlap.** Do two roles need nearly identical context and reasoning?
4. **Tool boundary.** Does a role need tools another role should not have?
5. **Human boundary.** Have I stated what the workforce may not decide or execute?
