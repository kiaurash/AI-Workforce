# Facilitator Blocker Attestation

Use this only when a learner is blocked by something outside their control and cannot produce direct repository evidence, such as an organizational policy, an account permission the learner cannot inspect, or an application failure before the repository is writable. See Course.md section 4.1.

What an attestation does:

- It is evidence that the blocker exists. With the learner's attempted recovery it satisfies the blocked-state gate and opens the catch-up route.

What it does not do:

- It is never evidence that a run succeeded, that an artifact exists, or that a control works.
- It does not replace the learner's own attempted recovery.

```markdown
# Facilitator Blocker Attestation

Learner: [...]
Module: [...]
Date and time: [...]
Facilitator: [...]

## What I observed
[the error text, the screen, or the behavior, as seen]

## How I observed it
[in person | shared screen | screenshot the learner provided | other] 

## Why direct repository evidence could not be produced
[the restriction or failure, and why it is outside the learner's control]

## Learner's attempted recovery (required)
[what the learner tried, and the result]

## What this attestation does not show
Execution status: unverified
No artifact is attested by this note.

## Follow-up
Catch-up route: [clinic | alternative environment | labeled facilitator demonstration | other]
Next check: [date]
Learner acknowledged: [yes / no]
```
